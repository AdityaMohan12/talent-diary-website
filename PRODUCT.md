# Talent Diary

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
A startup-specialist recruitment partner in India (talentdiary.in). We fill niche tech and non-tech roles for high-growth startups with a deeply vetted shortlist in under 30 days, not a flood of resumes. The site's single job: get a founder or hiring manager to book a hiring call.

## Users
- **Primary: startup founders and hiring managers** with an open role they cannot afford to fill wrong. Small teams, fast pace, every hire moves the needle. They trust-screen a recruiting partner in the first few seconds and have seen every generic staffing pitch. They reward relief and proof, not capability boasts.
- **Secondary: startup candidates** who join the talent network. Served by one quiet side-door, never allowed to compete with the hiring CTA.

## Positioning
The recruitment partner run by people who built the hiring engines at India's best-known startups (Unacademy, Interview Kickstart, 91Springboard, Awign). Not an "AI recruitment platform." Not a staffing mill. Operators who have hired at scale.

## The single promise
A shortlist of deeply vetted, startup-ready candidates in under 30 days.

## Big idea
"Every hire matters." One open role is treated as the high-stakes decision it is for a small team.

## Brand / Tone
Decisive, warm-professional, kinetic. Operator-to-operator, second person, outcome-first. Proof and people before technology. Reads like ex-Unacademy operators, never like a cheap template. Confidence without hype.

## Voice rules
- Second person, outcome-first. Lead with the founder's relief, not our capability.
- Reframe technology as a felt benefit, never name "agentic AI" before its benefit.
- No buzzwords, no praise adjectives. Specific facts only.
- No em dashes anywhere (use commas, colons, periods, parentheses).

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)
- Generic corporate recruitment: stock handshake photos, smiling-candidate imagery, timid corporate blue accents.
- SaaS-template landing: centered hero stack, icon-title-subtitle card grid, gradient text.
- Editorial-magazine affectation: display-serif italic + track-spaced mono labels + ruled three-column grid.
- Uncanny AI/stock faces (PFD L1 trust-killer for this discerning audience).

## Strategic principles
- Trust is a perceptual output, not an argument. Visual quality must match an ex-operator price point before any claim is read.
- Numbers before claims. Proof bar numerals are the largest non-headline type on the page.
- Founder pedigree is the strongest asset; surface it early (hero micro-proof) and in full (founders section).
- Demonstrate, don't describe: show a shortlist, show the 3-step mechanism.
